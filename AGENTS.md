# FoodieAgent Guidelines for Antigravity

When working with food and dining recommendations in this repository:
1. **Always Use Google AI Mode Format:**
   - Present recommendations using structured cards with ratings, review count, price tier, road distance with driving time, and full street addresses.
   - Include direct action links for `[🧭 Directions]`, `[📞 Call]`, and `[🌐 Google Maps]`.
   - Provide the three curated insight sections: `💫 The Vibe`, `🎯 Why It Wins`, and `💡 Insider Tip`.
2. **Location First:**
   - Always confirm the user's location before searching.
   - For Phagwara / LPU / Athouli / Law Gate / Chaheru, prioritize verified local favorites (e.g., Deccan Dose, Alam Muradabadi, Food Station, Little India LPU, A-One Biryani Corner).
3. **No Hallucination:**
   - Never invent nonexistent restaurants or incorrect distance numbers. If a specific niche tag is not on the map, provide the closest top-rated dining spots and a direct Google Maps search link.
